Deleting your JetSIM account and data

You can delete your account and all personal data associated with it directly inside the JetSIM app — no email or waiting period required.

Open the app, go to Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete account". Confirm when asked. The deletion happens immediately.

What gets deleted

Your account, your sign-in identity (Apple, Google, or Facebook), your email address and name, your support conversations, and the link between you and your past orders are permanently removed.

We keep anonymised purchase records with no link to you, because tax and accounting law requires us to retain transaction records. These cannot be traced back to you.

What is not affected

eSIMs you have already installed keep working until their plan expires — an installed eSIM lives on your device's hardware, not in your JetSIM account. You will no longer be able to see or manage them from the app.

Payment records held by Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider and merchant of record, are governed by their own retention policy. Contact them directly for those.

If you cannot access the app

Email jetsupport@travelesimpro.com from the address linked to your account and ask for deletion. We will verify ownership and delete the account within 30 days.
